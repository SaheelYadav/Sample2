The founder describes herself as an artist by birth, a graphic designer by choice and an art teacher by destiny. Her supplied profile mentions a postgraduate degree in Media and Communication, work with Jaya TV and Vijay TV, 3D graphics, and teaching graphic design at Loyola College and Mindtree.

Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Based on the FAQ and class information in the supplied Lumiza document.

Who can join Lumiza Creations?

The supplied FAQ says classes are open to learners aged 5 to 60, from complete beginners to experienced artists.

Do I need prior art experience?

No. The supplied document says classes cater to different skill levels, with guidance to help learners progress at their own pace.

Are classes online?

Yes. The document describes online sessions, regular classes, one-on-one sessions, group sessions, workshops and summer camps.

How often are regular classes held?

The supplied document describes regular classes twice a week. Contact Lumiza to confirm current times and time-zone options.

What materials do I need?

Lumiza says the team will suggest required materials and share purchasing links. Online learners should confirm the list before class.

What if I miss a class?

The supplied FAQ says a make-up session may be arranged. Rescheduling depends on availability and current policy.

Are discounts available?

The supplied document mentions possible group-booking discounts and packages. Ask Lumiza for current prices and offers.
